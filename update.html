BetterLET

Connect your BetterLET folder

Chrome does not tell extensions where they are stored, so you pick the folder one time. BetterLET checks that it is the right one, and it can only touch that folder.

  1. Open chrome://extensions and click Details on BetterLET.
  2. Look under Source. That is your folder.
  3. Click Choose folder below, pick it, and click Allow.

Chrome will not allow the top of Documents, Downloads, or Desktop. If BetterLET sits there, move it into its own folder and load it again. You can skip this and do it later from the gear.

Firefox does not let an add-on replace its own files. Download the zip, unzip it over your BetterLET folder, then click Reload on BetterLET in about:debugging.
